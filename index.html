Two

My letter to you

Read it slowly. The words light up as you go.

Dear Rian,

I do not always know how to put these heavy, quiet feelings into words, so I am saying them here slowly, where my trembling heart will not hold me back.

Before you, I believed happiness was something you chase endlessly, a distant destination you might never reach. You taught me that happiness is something shared, it lives inside the gentle presence of someone who sees every broken part of you and chooses to stay anyway.

You turned the meaning of home into a person rather than a place. When everything around me falls apart, I no longer crave silence, I crave your presence, because you are the calm that holds my whole world together.

I am far from perfect, and I will never pretend to have it all figured out. Still, I will keep choosing you through every storm and every peaceful day, within the unseen small moments and ordinary days that make our story ours. That is where real love truly resides.

Thank you for loving every unpolished piece of me, for holding space for my weariness, and for never walking away when I need you the most.

Mahal kita. It is the deepest truth inside my soul, and I needed to tell you this properly.

Six

A last whisper

The stars were here long before us, and they will keep burning long after. Look up at the sky behind this page, let it be our witness, and then make a wish.

If every star forgets how to shine,
I will still find my way to you.
Through every night, through every silence,
through every version of this world,
my heart will keep your name
the way the sky keeps its light.
Mahal kita, hanggang sa huling bituin.
